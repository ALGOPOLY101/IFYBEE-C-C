ABOUT THE ACADEMY

Where passion meets professional baking.

Ifybee's Academy is a creative learning space for people who want to build confidence in baking, decorating and crafting beautiful desserts.

Our goal is to help learners understand the fundamentals, explore new techniques and turn their interest in baking into useful, real-world skills.

We focus on practical learning that balances instruction with hands-on application, giving beginners a strong foundation while leaving room for experienced bakers to grow.

FAQ

Everything you need to know.

Who can join the Academy?

The Academy is designed for beginners, hobby bakers and aspiring pastry creators.

Do beginners need previous experience?

No. Beginner-friendly classes are designed to introduce the fundamentals step by step.

What should I bring?

Students may be asked to bring items such as a notebook, apron or personal tools depending on the class.

How long are the classes?

Duration depends on the course and may range from individual sessions to multi-week programmes.

Are online classes available?

Learning formats will depend on the specific class and programme schedule.
